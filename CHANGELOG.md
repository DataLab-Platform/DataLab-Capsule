# Changelog

All notable changes to DataLab-Capsule are documented in this file. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). The capsule format itself is versioned by its profile (<https://datalab-platform.com/capsule/0.1>).

## [Unreleased]

First public release (0.1.0), shared by DataLab Desktop and DataLab-Web.

### Added

- **Workspace provenance model:** a neutral record of processing (operation calls with their parameters, input and output states, activities, execution environments) stored in DataLab workspaces (`/DataLab_Provenance`) and read and written with h5py alone. Signal and image states are identified by fingerprints of their data, coordinates or calibration and units, and by their regions of interest; analysis results by the digest of their stored value. A state whose saved data no longer match is reported as altered.
- **Replay preparation and verification reports:** an edition can recompute a recorded activity and compare it with the stored result (`exact`, `within_tolerance` under the tolerance declared for the operation, `different`, or `not_verified` with its reason); only operations with a qualified contract are replayed, and nothing named in a file is ever executed. An input whose data are unchanged but whose region of interest changed since the activity (for example regions added by a detection) is replayed on a copy carrying the recorded region of interest, never modified itself. A recorded result whose data or region of interest changed since the activity is reported as altered and never used as reference.
- **Provenance of a result:** an edition can describe, for one signal or image, the recorded activity that produced its state (parameters, ordered inputs and their ancestors, expanded on demand), what is still available, the analyses recorded on it and whether the local library knows and qualifies the operation, without computing, and check the data of the object and of the direct inputs against the recorded states. Processing restores the call that produced the latest recorded state of an object, every input included; knowing an operation and qualifying its verification are kept apart. When a workspace file is appended to the current one, its provenance is not imported, and objects holding an identifier already known to the current ledger never inherit what was recorded for another object.
- **Legacy workspaces:** processing records stored by earlier DataLab versions become legacy activities that keep the record as stored, invent nothing and are never replayed.
- **Capsules (`.dlcapsule`):** a ZIP archive with the workspace and an RO-Crate 1.3 manifest (Process Run Crate 0.6 and the DataLab capsule profile 0.1). The safe reader refuses path traversal, links, duplicates, encrypted, compressed or damaged entries and oversized archives, and never accesses the network. A capsule may reference its workspace by an address instead of including it; the bytes supplied must match the manifest.
- **Revisions and reviews:** a capsule may name its parent revision; reviews are separate documents about one revision, with their own fingerprint. The capsule a workspace was opened from is saved with the workspace file, so that a revision exported after the workspace was saved and opened again still names its parent.
- **Light attachments:** up to 8 files of 16 MiB (protocol, notes, small tables) declared in the manifest with their size and SHA-256; checked, returned as data, never opened or executed.
- **Comparison (`diff`) and verification (`verify`):** changes between two revisions sorted into scientific, presentation, storage, environment and attachment changes, with a scientific conclusion that never claims equality beyond what the manifests record; verification of the archive, the workspace, the stored states and supplied reports and reviews, without computation.
- **Command line `workspace-capsule`:** `validate`, `inspect`, `diff`, `verify`, `attachment`, `workspace`, `create`, `review`, and `registry list | publish | fetch`.
- **Optional registry (`registry` extra):** publishes and serves manifests, their attachments and reviews (never workspaces), with checks on every document, an OpenAPI description, open reading and token-protected publishing (`workspace-capsule-registry`). Its client reads each response up to a size limit and checks everything it fetches; it also gives the registry status and each publishing step apart (manifest, attachment, review), and downloads an attachment checked against its manifest.
- **Capsule interfaces (`exchange`):** inspection, opening, attachment download, registry checks, comparison and reviews as offered by the capsule dialogs of DataLab Desktop and DataLab-Web, so that both editions give the same answers. Supplied reports and reviews are checked apart from the integrity of the capsule.
- Runs on CPython 3.9 to 3.14 and in Pyodide (registry excepted).
- **Measured costs:** benchmarks of fingerprints and capsules (`benchmarks/`, in CPython and in Pyodide) with one command line and one report format shared with the benchmarks of DataLab Desktop and DataLab-Web; method, results and recommended sizes in [doc/performance.md](doc/performance.md).
- **Release checks:** the distributions are checked to carry every module (including `exchange`) and, on release tags, schemas and context with the SHA-256 published by the profile page (`scripts/check_profile.py`, also usable offline against a checkout of the profile).
